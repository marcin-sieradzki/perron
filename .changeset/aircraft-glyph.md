Adds the aircraft for a map that draws the planes over the city: the `aircraft` icon, a plane seen from above pointing north, on the 14 px grid at `icon.size.325`; `color.map.aircraft`, a magenta apart from every line, mode, status and the brand, at 3:1 or more on every map ground in both themes; `map.aircraft-size.air` and `map.aircraft-size.ground` for the glyph's box, and `map.aircraft-stroke-width.ageing` and `map.aircraft-stroke-width.estimated` for its rims, with the data states drawn as the Map page's Aircraft section says. VehicleDotLegend gains `aircraftVisible`, which adds the glyph beside "Planes" after the five states, and `aircraftLabel`, which overrides that word.
