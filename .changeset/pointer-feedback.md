Button answers the pointer: a pointer cursor (default while disabled), a hover step the eye sees, and a pressed step past it. Adds `color.surface.inverse-pressed`, `color.surface.brand-pressed`, `palette.neutral.400` and `palette.yellow.550`. `color.surface.inverse-hover` moves to `palette.neutral.700` in light and `color.surface.brand-hover` to `palette.yellow.550` in light, so hover shows on the components that use them. The contract's Pointer feedback rule asks the same of every part a pointer presses.
