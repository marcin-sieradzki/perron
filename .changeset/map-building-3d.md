Map tokens for 3D buildings: `color.map.building-3d` (light `#D3D9D4`, dark `#2A3338`), the one colour an extruded building's walls and roof take, and `opacity.map-building-3d` (`0.6`), so the street behind a block shows through. `docs/foundations/map.md` gains a "3D buildings" section: buildings rise from zoom 14, heights come from OpenStreetMap and are approximate, and the map opens flat with 3D as a view the rider turns on.
