# Sheet

A panel that slides in from the edge of the screen and floats over the map: the overview sheet and the detail panel on a wide screen, and the bottom sheet they become on a phone ([Layout](../foundations/layout.md#page-shell)).

The design is deferred (`docs/adr/0010-unattended-runs-with-design-deferred.md`): this spec decides the variant property, the parts and the token of every part, from the contract, the foundations, the tokens and the Base UI reference. A later design run can change them.

## Anatomy

Penpot page `Components/Sheet`. Family: `Panels`. Shared parts: `CloseButton`.

| Part (Penpot layer) | What it is                                                                                          | Base UI part                                                    | Class               | `slotProps` key |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------- |
| `root`              | The panel itself: the dialog, holding the header and the content                                    | `Drawer.Popup`                                                  | `pn-Sheet`          | none            |
| `trigger`           | The caller's button that opens the sheet, when the caller gives one                                 | `Drawer.Trigger`                                                | none: the caller's  | `trigger`       |
| `portal`            | Moves the sheet to the end of `<body>`, or to the caller's container; draws nothing                 | `Drawer.Portal`                                                 | none                | `portal`        |
| `backdrop`          | The scrim behind a modal sheet; not rendered when the sheet is not modal                            | `Drawer.Backdrop`                                               | `pn-Sheet-backdrop` | `backdrop`      |
| `viewport`          | The fixed layer that places the panel against its edge, `size.panel-gap` in from the screen's edges | `Drawer.Viewport`                                               | `pn-Sheet-viewport` | `viewport`      |
| `header`            | The row along the top of the panel: the title, then the close button                                | none: a `div` that only lays out the title and the close button | `pn-Sheet-header`   | none            |
| `title`             | The sheet's name, the heading that labels the dialog                                                | `Drawer.Title`                                                  | `pn-Sheet-title`    | `title`         |
| `close`             | The icon button that closes the sheet, the `CloseButton` shared part                                | `Drawer.Close`                                                  | `pn-Sheet-close`    | `close`         |
| `content`           | The caller's children, below the header; it scrolls on its own, so the header stays in place        | `Drawer.Content`                                                | `pn-Sheet-content`  | `content`       |

`Drawer.Description` is not one of the sheet's parts: `packages/perron/components.json` leaves it out, and a sheet's body is the caller's own content.

Base UI already does: the `dialog` role and its name from the title through `aria-labelledby`; `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the trigger; moving focus into the sheet on open (the first tabbable element, or the popup) and back to the trigger or the element focused before on close; trapping focus, locking the page's scroll and hiding everything outside from assistive technology with `aria-hidden` while modal; closing the topmost sheet on Esc; closing a modal sheet on a press outside it; swipe to dismiss in `swipeDirection`, with `data-swiping`, `--drawer-swipe-movement-x`, `--drawer-swipe-movement-y` and `--drawer-swipe-strength`; snapping a bottom sheet to its snap points, with `--drawer-snap-point-offset`; `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style` for the open and close transitions; letting a mouse select text inside `Drawer.Content` without starting a swipe; and the `hidden` attribute on a closed popup kept mounted.

A bottom sheet is opaque because [Elevation](../foundations/elevation.md#surfaces) puts panels full of text on `color.surface.default` on phones, where bottom sheets are.
- The `close` rows belong to the `CloseButton` part: the sheet places it, and the part draws it (CR-11).
- The sheet's geometry: a side sheet is `size.sheet-width` wide and the viewport's full height, `size.panel-gap` from the screen's edges; a bottom sheet is the viewport's full width, flush with the bottom and side edges, and at most `size.panel-gap` short of the top; the header is padded `space.400` at the top and sides with `space.300` between the title and the close button; the content sits `space.300` below the header and is padded `space.400` at the sides and bottom, as the rows above give them.
- Side sheets slide in and out on `duration.sheet` and `easing.sheet` too, though the tokens' descriptions name only the bottom sheet, so every sheet moves alike ([Motion](../foundations/motion.md): "The sheet slides").
- The `CloseButton` part has no folder yet, so Sheet, the first component that composes it, decides its look: an icon button `size.control` square with `radius.md`, the `close` icon at `icon.size.400` (its size in `packages/perron/icons/icons.json`) in `color.text.secondary`, turning `color.text.primary` on `color.surface.hover` on hover and `color.surface.selected` when pressed, as the `close` rows above give it.

## Keyboard and ARIA

From the Base UI reference for each part. "From" says who does it: Base UI, or Perron.

| Key                | Does                                                                                                                               | From                                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `Enter`, `Space`   | On the trigger, opens the sheet and moves focus into it: the first tabbable element, the close button, or the popup                | Base UI                                               |
| `Enter`, `Space`   | On the close button, closes the sheet with reason `close-press`, and focus returns to the trigger or to the element focused before | Base UI                                               |
| `Esc`              | Closes the topmost open sheet with reason `escape-key`, and focus returns as above                                                 | Base UI                                               |
| `Tab`, `Shift+Tab` | In a modal sheet, moves through the sheet's tabbable elements and wraps inside it                                                  | Base UI                                               |
| `Tab`, `Shift+Tab` | In a sheet that is not modal, moves on into the page; the sheet stays open                                                         | Base UI, with `disablePointerDismissal` set by Perron |

| Element    | Role                 | Accessible name from                      | States announced                                                                 |
| ---------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `trigger`  | `button`             | The caller's element                      | `aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, with `aria-controls` naming the popup |
| `root`     | `dialog`             | `title`, through `aria-labelledby`        | While modal, everything outside it is `aria-hidden`                              |
| `title`    | `heading`, level 2   | `title`                                   | None                                                                             |
| `close`    | `button`             | The owned `close` string, or `closeLabel` | None                                                                             |
| `backdrop` | None: presentational | None                                      | None                                                                             |

What axe won't catch, from the contract, each with how this component meets it:

- [ ] Everything the eye is told, the ear is told, for every shape each prop accepts: the close button's icon is `aria-hidden` and the `CloseButton` part gives it the owned label; the grab and snap of a bottom sheet have the close button and Esc as their equivalent for keys and screen readers.
- [ ] Every landmark and named region has an accessible name: the dialog is named by its required `title`, through Base UI's `aria-labelledby`.
- [ ] Every default label can be overridden: `closeLabel`.
- [ ] Every shape the type accepts renders, in every variant: `title` and `children` show a string, an element and a list alike, in each `anchor`.
- [ ] A hidden copy kept for animation is `aria-hidden`, and a collapsed region is `inert`: none here; a closed sheet is unmounted, or `hidden` when `slotProps.portal.keepMounted` keeps it.
- [ ] Native semantics first: `<button>` for the trigger and the close button, `<h2>` for the title, and Base UI's `dialog` role on the root.
- [ ] Focus moves in reading order and returns on close: title, close button, then the content; Base UI returns focus to the trigger or to the element focused before.
- [ ] Every target is at least `size.target-min` square: the close button, `size.control`.
- [ ] Loops stop when the system asks for less motion: none here; the slide reads `duration.sheet`, which the tokens shorten to 0.01 ms.

## Component rules

Rules for this component alone, numbered so the build and the review can cite them.

- **CR-1.** Base UI's Drawer already handles focus moving in and back, the focus trap and scroll lock of a modal sheet, Esc, outside presses, swipes and snap points, so the component rebuilds none of them.
- **CR-2.** The root is `Drawer.Popup`: `ref`, `className` (`pn-Sheet` first, then the caller's) and the rest props reach it, the rest spread before the component's own attributes. The other parts are reached only through `slotProps`, merged with `mergeProps`, the component's own props last.
- **CR-3.** `anchor` maps to `swipeDirection`: `left` to `'left'`, `right` to `'right'`, `bottom` to `'down'`. The root and the viewport carry `data-anchor` with its value, the default included, and the CSS places and slides the sheet from it: `.pn-Sheet[data-anchor='bottom']`.
- **CR-4.** While `modal` is false, the component passes `disablePointerDismissal`, renders no `Drawer.Backdrop`, and the viewport takes `pointer-events: none` with the root back on `auto`, so the map under and beside the sheet stays usable. While `modal` is true, the backdrop renders with `color.scrim`, and a press on it closes the sheet with reason `outside-press`.
- **CR-5.** The viewport and the backdrop take `z-index.dialog`, as everything in a portal does. A pattern that lays a sheet among the map screen's panels sets its own `z-index.*` through `slotProps.viewport`'s `className`.
- **CR-6.** The component holds no media query for the phone: it sizes to its viewport, and the screen picks `anchor` `bottom` under `breakpoint.phone`.
- **CR-7.** The root is a flex column: the header keeps its height, and the content takes the rest, with `overflow-y: auto` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`, so a long body scrolls under a header that stays.
- **CR-8.** A side sheet moves with `transform: translateX(var(--drawer-swipe-movement-x))`, and a bottom sheet with `transform: translateY(calc(var(--drawer-snap-point-offset) + var(--drawer-swipe-movement-y)))`. Under `[data-starting-style]` and `[data-ending-style]` it sits off its edge by its own size. Base UI's `--drawer-*` properties are read, never set, by the component's CSS.
- **CR-9.** The snap point props reach `Drawer.Root` only with `anchor` `bottom`. With no `snapPoints`, a bottom sheet snaps to `size.sheet-peek`, read as a number from `tokens.ts`, and to `1`, and opens at the peek.
- **CR-10.** `onOpen(event, details)` and `onClose(event, reason, details)` are adapted from `onOpenChange` inside the component, with `event` as `details.event` and `reason` as `details.reason`; `onSnapPointChange(event, snapPoint, details)` is adapted the same way. Neither of Base UI's callbacks is exposed.
- **CR-11.** The close button is the `CloseButton` shared part, rendered through `Drawer.Close`, after the title in the header. Its label is `useOwnedStrings({ close: closeLabel }).close`. When the part's folder does not exist yet, the build adds it, as the contract's [Shared parts](README.md#shared-parts) say, drawn by the `close` rows of [Tokens](#tokens), with the `close` icon from `packages/perron/icons/close.svg`.
- **CR-12.** The portal renders nothing in static markup, so the contract's `OwnedStrings` story sees only the trigger. A `play` test runs `expectOwnedStrings` over the open sheet's markup in `da`, with the story's `title` and `children` as the caller's words.
- **CR-13.** The `Left` story's args hold `defaultOpen: true`, so the contract story finds its root, the popup, with the `ref` and the `data-testid` on it.

## Do and don't

For a caller using the component, with the reason.

| Do                                                                                                                                                    | Don't                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Use a sheet for a panel beside the map, left open while the rider pans and picks vehicles: it is not modal by default                                 | Use a modal sheet to ask for a decision; that is a Dialog, which needs no swipe or snap points (Base UI's own advice)                              |
| Pick `anchor` `bottom` on a phone, under `breakpoint.phone`, in the screen's code                                                                     | Expect the sheet to turn into a bottom sheet on its own; it sizes to its viewport and holds no media query                                         |
| Name the sheet with `title`: the stop, line or destination it shows                                                                                   | Leave the title out, or draw a heading of your own in `children`: the dialog takes its name from `title`                                           |
| Handle `onClose` and let Esc close the topmost sheet, as [Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#rules) orders: a dialog, then a detail panel | Cancel every close of a sheet the rider can't otherwise leave; cancel only the reasons a screen must refuse, such as `swipe` on the overview sheet |
| Mark a horizontal carousel or slider inside a bottom sheet with `data-base-ui-swipe-ignore`, so its drags don't swipe the sheet                       | Let a control that drags sideways fight the sheet's swipe                                                                                          |
| Keep one primary action per sheet                                                                                                                     | Put two brand buttons in one sheet; a screen has one brand action at most                                                                          |
