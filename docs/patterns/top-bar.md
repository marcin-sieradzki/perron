# TopBar

The bar along the top of Mylder's moving map, on every screen size: the Mylder brand, the status line that says when the map's data is from, the screen's filters and its tools, on one glass panel over the map.

<!-- run notes -->

The design is deferred (ADR 0010, `docs/adr/0010-unattended-runs-with-design-deferred.md`): this spec decides what the drawing would, from the [contract](../components/README.md) and its Patterns section, the [foundations](../foundations/README.md), [Layout](../foundations/layout.md), [Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md), [content design](../content-design.md), the tokens, and the specs of the components the bar composes or holds: [Surface](../components/surface.md), [Brand](../components/brand.md), [Text](../components/text.md), [FilterChip](../components/filter-chip.md), [IconButton](../components/icon-button.md) and [LinkButton](../components/link-button.md), with [SourceLine](source-line.md), whose words the status line matches, and [KioskBar](kiosk-bar.md), the other bar over the map. Its ROADMAP line is ticket marcin-sieradzki/perron#159, part of #150: the brand, a status line ("Updated 08:14:05", with a paused state), the mode filters as FilterChips with counts, and the tools (language, About), in glass over the map, laid out for phone and desktop with `size.top-bar`. The ticket carries Mylder's own composition of the bar, from Surface, Brand, Text, FilterChip, IconButton and LinkButton, and asks that it can be swapped for this pattern without a visual change, Mylder keeping its data, its filter state and its language links and passing them in: that composition is the reference this spec follows for every measure it writes (Mylder's own padding loses to Surface's glass by specificity, see [Tokens](#tokens)), and nothing here rests on the extracted prototype, which is not reachable from this branch. SourceLine's run left a note that this status line should compose SourceLine or match it; it matches it (see [Props](#props)). No sibling is on a branch.

## Anatomy

Penpot page `Patterns/TopBar`. Family: Panels, since it is a panel floating over the map and takes the Panels row's glass through [Surface](../components/surface.md)'s `glass` variant. Shared parts: none of its own; the `VisuallyHidden` parts inside Brand, FilterChip and IconButton stay inside them.

The bar is a wrapping flex row of three groups: the `identity` (the brand, and the status line beside it or under it), the `filters` and the `tools`. On a wide screen it is one row, identity, filters, then tools at the end; on a phone, `size='small'`, the status line sits under the brand, the tools stay on the first row at its end, and the filters take a second row of their own, scrolling sideways when they don't fit. It is Mylder's composition as the ticket gives it.

The drawing will have a `root` board holding `identity`, `filters` and `tools`, with `brand` and `status` inside `identity`, and `paused`, `separator` and `time` inside `status`. Its variant properties are `size` and `State`, in that order. `State` is the interaction state of the one part the pattern draws that a pointer presses, the `brand` when `brandHref` makes it a link (`default`, `hover` and `pressed`, mapped to `:hover` and `:active` on `.pn-TopBar-brand[href]` in the code map), as the Conventions of `penpot/README.md` keep interaction states in a `State` property; everything else is at rest. The brand's looks are the same in both sizes, so the drawing's variants are `MediumDefault`, `MediumHover`, `MediumPressed` and `SmallDefault`. The chips and buttons the caller puts in `filters` and `tools` keep their own states.

The root is a plain `div` on Surface's glass, not a `header` landmark, and the screen wraps the bar in the `header` its page needs, as KioskBar leaves its landmark to its screen. A pattern can't take `render` to swap its element (see [Base UI parts](#base-ui-parts)); the contract's checklist asks every landmark for an accessible name, which a `header` would need from a new owned string or from the brand; and a `header` inside the `main` or `section` a story or a screen renders it in is no banner but a generic element, on which an `aria-label` is prohibited. Mylder's composition renders its Surface as a `header`, so Mylder wraps the bar in one, which changes nothing on screen.

The bar is one glass Surface holding every part, as Mylder's composition has it, not a glass group for each part, as Layout's page shell words it ("a top bar of glass groups"). The ticket asks that Mylder's composition can be swapped for the pattern without a visual change, and that composition is one glass panel; one panel also gives the filters one ground to scroll over on a phone.

`className`, `style`, `ref` and the other `div` props reach the root, as the contract's [Root](../components/README.md#root) says. Every prop the table declares is taken out of the rest props first; the props type leaves out `children`, since the bar writes its own parts. No `variant` or `color`: the bar has one look.

TopBar takes a `size` of `'medium' | 'small'`, default `'medium'`, that the screen sets to `small` under `breakpoint.phone`, in place of Mylder's two media queries at 760 px. The contract allows a component only three media queries and has it size to its container, and no CSS query can read `breakpoint.phone`; DetailPanel and the Sheet leave the phone to their screen the same way, through `anchor`; [Layout](../foundations/layout.md#breakpoints) says the top bar "shrinks to `size.top-bar-phone`" under `breakpoint.phone`, which is a size, and the contract's `size` takes `small` and `medium` with `medium` the default. The root carries `data-size`.

TopBar takes its filters and its tools as the caller's elements, the `filters` and `tools` slots, not as data it renders into FilterChips, an IconButton and a LinkButton, so its `dependsOn` is `Surface`, `Brand` and `Text`. The ticket keeps the filter state, the counts and the language links with Mylder, and each chip's `selected` and `onChange` are FilterChip's own, which a slot passes through whole where data would need an event of the bar's own for every chip; Mylder's About button will open a dialog, whose trigger is the caller's element; its language link needs the page's query string, read in a Suspense boundary of Mylder's own; and DetailHeader and DataStatePanel take their `action` as a slot the same way. A slot also keeps Mylder's own elements, so the swap changes nothing on screen.

The status line matches SourceLine rather than composing it: its own `time`, `paused` and `outOfDate` props, SourceLine's words and order ("Paused · Updated 08:14:05", "Updated 08:09:40, may be out of date"), its separator and its inks, but no source name and Mylder's `typography.data-sm`. SourceLine requires a source name, which the top bar's line has none of ("Updated 08:14:05", the ticket's words), and sets its line in `typography.caption`, where Mylder's composition sets it in `typography.data-sm`, so composing it would change what Mylder shows; matching its states, words and inks keeps "every panel's source line says it the same way" (Mylder's screens), as SourceLine's note asks.

`time` is optional: while it is left out the line reads "Updated —", the "—" filling `{time}` as text, with no `dateTime`. Mylder shows "Updated —" before its first answer, and content design writes "—" for unknown. The "—" is text, not the `UnknownValue` helper's hidden "—" with "Unknown" for the ear, because `expectOwnedStrings` can't read an owned phrase whose placeholder is an element, the gap SourceLine's run named; a screen reader reads the dash as its punctuation setting names it.

TopBar takes a `brandHref` and renders the Brand as `<a href={brandHref}>` when it is given, and the brand alone when it is not. Mylder's brand is a link to its start screen, `/${lang}`; a pattern can't take `render` to give it one; and Brand's own spec draws it as a link through `render`, keeping its `size.target-min` height.

The caller gives the time as a string and the bar runs no timer of its own, as SourceLine and KioskBar take theirs: `PerronProvider` gives a component its strings but not its time zone, so a clock inside the pattern would read the device's zone rather than Copenhagen's, and a timer would make the server's render and the first client render disagree.

TopBar takes Brand's `brandNameLabel` and gives it on, as KioskBar does, and reuses SourceLine's `sourcePaused` key, so its override is `sourcePausedLabel`: the contract's checklist asks that every default label the pattern shows can be overridden on one instance, and a key's override is named after the key.

## Tokens

One row per part, state and property. The one part TopBar draws that a pointer presses is the `brand` as a link, with `brandHref`: it takes the contract's [Pointer feedback](../components/README.md#pointer-feedback) for a quiet part, `color.surface.hover` on hover and `color.surface.selected` while pressed, on a `radius.md` box padded `space.100` at its sides, which a negative margin takes back so the lockup stays where it was; every other row is rest. The caller's chips, buttons and links keep their own tokens, and Brand and Text theirs. The glass is Surface's (`color.surface.overlay`, the `effect.glass-blur` and `effect.glass-saturate` backdrop, a `border.width.025` edge in `color.border.subtle`, `radius.xl`, `shadow.panel`), read from [Surface](../components/surface.md#tokens) and not set again here; the pattern sets only its padding, as the contract's Glass line allows. That padding is the measure Mylder's composition writes, not the one it renders: Mylder's `.bar` rule, one class, loses its `padding-block` and `padding-inline` to Surface's `.pn-Surface[data-variant='glass']`, a class and an attribute, so Mylder's bar renders with `space.400` on every side, 68 px tall on a wide screen where it means 46 px. TopBar's own rules tie with Surface's and come after it, so they win.

| `tools`    | rest                      | `display`                               | none: `flex`, with `align-items: center`                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `tools`    | rest                      | `gap`                                   | `space.100`                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `tools`    | rest                      | `margin-inline-start`                   | none: `auto`, so the tools end the row, or the line they wrap to                                                                                                                                           |

Every measure is the one Mylder's composition writes: the groups `space.300` apart and wrapped rows `space.100` apart, the bar `space.400` in from its ends on a wide screen and `space.300` on a phone, the chips `space.200` apart, or `space.100` on a phone, and the tools `space.100` apart. Mylder's bar doesn't render its padding, which loses to Surface's glass as above, so the swap brings Mylder's bar to the 46 px it means, `size.top-bar`, from the 68 px it shows, and on a phone to 90 px from 114 px, which is Mylder's intent. Beyond the height, the bar's measures and the brand link's box change what Mylder shows in these ways, and in no other: on a phone the bar is `space.300`, 12 px, in from its ends, where Mylder's renders 16 px, so the brand, the status line, the tools and the chips sit 4 px nearer the bar's edges, and the filters' scrolling box, which keeps room for a chip's focus ring, clips a scrolled chip 4 px nearer the bar's edges too; and the brand link's box is padded `space.100` at each side with `radius.md` corners, taken back by its margin, so the lockup stays where it was but the link fills with `color.surface.hover` under the pointer and `color.surface.selected` while pressed, its focus ring is drawn around that box, 8 px wider than the lockup, with rounded corners, where Mylder's hugs the lockup with square ones, and its hit area reaches 4 px further at each side. A `medium` bar's `space.100` block padding goes past what Mylder writes and changes nothing in a one-row bar. Three more rows go past it, and change nothing Mylder shows at its sizes: the chips wrap in a `medium` bar narrower than they are, where Mylder's would run past its edge; the status line breaks at its spaces when it is wider than the bar, where Mylder's `nowrap` would overflow a 320 px phone with the out-of-date phrase; and a `small` bar's filters keep room for a chip's focus ring above and at their sides, which Mylder's sideways scrolling clips.

The status line is set in `typography.data-sm` in `color.text.secondary` (Text's `variant='data-sm'` and `emphasis='secondary'`, as Mylder's composition sets it), with "Paused" in `color.text.primary` and the whole out-of-date phrase in `color.text.warning`, as SourceLine sets them, with no icon. `data-sm` is Mylder's and keeps the time's figures fixed in width as the seconds turn; SourceLine's inks make the two lines say a state alike, and the whole phrase takes the warning ink because one owned string can't be coloured in part without markup in the string, as SourceLine's run found. Every one of these inks reaches 4.5:1 on the opaque surfaces in both themes ([Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#text-on-surfaces)), and `color.surface.overlay` is `color.surface.default` at 0.9 and 0.88 alpha.

The root is block-level and sizes to its container, as the contract asks: TopBar places nothing, with no `position`, `inset`, outer `margin` or `z-index` of its own. The screen puts it over the map, `size.panel-gap` from the edges, at `z-index.top-bar` ([Layout](../foundations/layout.md#stacking)), as Mylder's composition does through its own class.

## Keyboard and ARIA

TopBar has no keys of its own. Tab moves through what the bar holds in the order it shows, which is the DOM order (CR-3): the brand's link when `brandHref` is given, then each chip in `filters` and then each control in `tools` in a `medium` bar, or the tools and then the filters in a `small` one. The chips, buttons and links answer their own keys, Enter and Space, as their specs say.

| Key         | Does                                                                                                                                                                       | From        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `Tab`       | Moves to the next focusable element in the bar, in the order on screen: the brand's link, then the filters and the tools, or with `size='small'` the tools and the filters | The browser |
| `Shift+Tab` | Moves back through the same elements, and out of the bar to whatever came before it                                                                                        | The browser |

| Element   | Role                                                     | Accessible name from                                          | States announced |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `root`    | None (generic `div`)                                     | None                                                          | None             |
| `brand`   | Link with `brandHref`; otherwise none (Brand's `span`)   | Its text: "Mylder", from the wordmark or Brand's hidden label | None             |
| `status`  | None (Text's `span`)                                     | None; its text is read in place                               | None             |
| `time`    | Time, the role ARIA gives the element                    | Its text, "Updated 08:14:05"                                  | None             |
| `filters` | Group                                                    | `aria-label`: the owned "Filters", or `filtersLabel`          | None             |
| `tools`   | None (generic `div`); the caller's controls carry theirs | The caller's controls' own names                              | Theirs           |

The filters' name is an `aria-label` on the `fieldset`, not a `legend`, as Mylder's composition names it: a `legend` renders on the fieldset's edge outside its flex row, and the `VisuallyHidden` part that would hide one is a `span`.

The status line is no live region: its time changes on every refresh, which would flood a screen reader, and [Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#what-a-person-checks) asks that only changes of state be announced, in the screen's own polite live region, as SourceLine's line is none.

What axe won't catch, from the contract, each with how this component meets it:

- [ ] Everything the eye is told, the ear is told, for every shape each prop accepts: the brand is heard as "Mylder" in both of Brand's variants and as a link with `brandHref`; the status line is its own text, "Paused · Updated 08:14:05" or "Updated 08:09:40, may be out of date", so the paused and out-of-date states are words, not only inks; the filters group is named, and each chip says its count and its pressed state itself. The unknown time's "—" is text, read as the screen reader names it.
- [ ] Every landmark and named region has an accessible name: the root is not a landmark, and the screen that wraps it in a `header` has its banner; the filters group is named "Filters", or by `filtersLabel`.
- [ ] Every default label can be overridden: `updatedLabel`, `updatedOutOfDateLabel`, `sourcePausedLabel`, `filtersLabel` and `brandNameLabel`.
- [ ] Every shape the type accepts renders, in every variant: `filters` and `tools` render a string, an element, a fragment and a list alike, and render no group for `null`, `false` or an empty list; `time` given as `HH:MM:SS`, as `HH:MM` and left out; `paused` and `outOfDate` alone and together; both sizes. `TopBar.test.tsx` renders each shape of `filters` and `tools`.
- [ ] A hidden copy kept for animation is `aria-hidden`, and a collapsed region is `inert`: none here; nothing animates or collapses.
- [ ] Native semantics first: a `fieldset` for the filters, a `time` for the time, an `<a href>` for the brand's link, plain `div` and `span` for the rest, and no ARIA of the pattern's own but the group's `aria-label`.
- [ ] Focus moves in reading order and returns on close: the DOM order is the order on screen in both sizes (CR-3), and nothing in the bar opens or closes.
- [ ] Every target is at least `size.target-min` square: the brand's link keeps Brand's `size.target-min` height; the chips and buttons the stories put in the bar are `size.control` and `size.button-sm` tall.
- [ ] Loops stop when the system asks for less motion: none here; nothing moves, and the time changes its text without a transition.

## Component rules

Rules for this pattern alone, numbered so the build and the review can cite them.

- **CR-1.** The pattern imports only `Surface`, `Brand`, `Text`, `PerronProvider`, the `classes`, `given` and `interpolate` helpers, React and the files of its own folder, as the contract's Patterns section says; never `@base-ui/react`. A type-only import of `BrandProps` is fine. `dependsOn` in `packages/perron/components.json` is `Surface`, `Brand` and `Text`.
- **CR-2.** The root is `<Surface variant="glass">`, its class `pn-TopBar` then the caller's joined after Surface's own, with `data-size` from `size`, `'medium'` included, and the caller's `ref`, `style` and other `div` props on it. The pattern never sets the glass's fill, blur, edge, radius or shadow itself; it sets only its padding (Tokens).
- **CR-3.** The groups are in one order, in the DOM and on screen: with `size='medium'`, `identity`, `filters`, then `tools`; with `size='small'`, `identity`, `tools`, then `filters`, so the tools stay on the first row, beside an identity that takes the room they leave (`flex: 1 1 0`), and the filters take the second, and Tab follows the order on screen in both, with no CSS `order`. Inside `identity`, the `brand` comes before the `status`.
- **CR-4.** The `status` is `<Text variant="data-sm" emphasis="secondary">`, holding, in order: the `paused` part and a `separator` when `paused` is set, then the `time` part. The `separator` has a no-break space (U+00A0) before it and a space after it, outside it, as text, so the line never starts a line with a "·", as SourceLine's CR-1 has it. The `time` is `<time dateTime={time}>` holding one owned phrase, `updated` or, with `outOfDate`, `updatedOutOfDate`, its `{time}` filled by `interpolate` with the caller's `time` as it is, or with "—" (U+2014) when `time` is left out, and then without `dateTime`. The phrase is one text run, never an owned word joined to the caller's time in code. The root carries the bare `data-paused` while `paused` is set and the bare `data-out-of-date` while `outOfDate` is set, and neither otherwise, as SourceLine's root carries `data-paused`.
- **CR-5.** The `brand` is `<Brand variant="full" brandNameLabel={brandNameLabel} />`, `slotProps.brand` laid under, so its `variant` wins over the default `'full'` and its class joins after `pn-TopBar-brand`; with `brandHref`, it takes `render={<a href={brandHref} />}`, and without it renders Brand's own `span`.
- **CR-6.** The pattern runs no timer, reads no clock and formats nothing: no `Date`, no "8 s ago", no count up from the time. The time changes only when the caller gives a new one, and the bar never decides `outOfDate` or `paused` from it: the app does.
- **CR-7.** The `filters` group renders only when `filters` renders something, and the `tools` group only when `tools` does, by the `given` helper, so no empty group is announced. The `filters` group is a `fieldset` with `aria-label` from `filtersLabel` over the owned "Filters". The pattern passes the caller's elements through as they are: it sets nothing on them and reads none of their state.
- **CR-8.** The pattern reads its words with `useOwnedStrings({ updated: updatedLabel, updatedOutOfDate: updatedOutOfDateLabel, sourcePaused: sourcePausedLabel, filters: filtersLabel })` from `packages/perron/src/PerronProvider.tsx`, and gives `brandNameLabel` to the Brand.
- **CR-9.** With `size='small'`, the `filters` row scrolls sideways (`overflow-x: auto`, `scrollbar-width: none`) and keeps `focus.width` plus `focus.offset` of room above and at its sides, taken back by a negative margin of the same length, and `space.100` below, so the focus ring perron.css draws around a chip is never clipped by the scrolling box, and the row's place in the bar is Mylder's.
- **CR-10.** At 320 px wide, in every locale, nothing in the bar reaches past its container: a `medium` bar wraps its groups and its chips, a `small` bar's filters scroll inside their row, and the status line breaks at its spaces only when it is wider than the bar (`overflow-wrap: break-word`), never inside the time.
- **CR-11.** The props type extends `Omit<ComponentPropsWithRef<'div'>, 'children'>` and declares every prop of [Props](#props), each taken out of the rest props before the spread, so none reaches the DOM. The root spreads the rest props onto Surface, then sets its own `variant`, `ref`, `data-size`, `data-paused`, `data-out-of-date` and class, `pn-TopBar` then the caller's `className` by the `classes` helper from `packages/perron/src/internal/nodes.ts`, so its own win.
- **CR-12.** Each inner part's props are its `slotProps` value with the pattern's own laid over it: the class joins the part's own first, then the caller's, by `classes`, and the children, `dateTime`, `aria-label`, `variant`, `emphasis`, `render` and `brandNameLabel` are the pattern's, but for `slotProps.brand`'s `variant`, which wins (CR-5). A pattern cannot import Base UI's `mergeProps`, so the merge is a spread, then the pattern's own props.
- **CR-13.** Neither the root nor any part is a live region, and nothing moves focus: no `aria-live`, `role="status"` or `role="log"`. A screen that must announce a change of state, such as the data feed failing, does it in its own polite live region.
- **CR-14.** TopBar places nothing: no `position`, `inset`, outer `margin` or `z-index` on the root. It sizes to its container, and its screen places it at `z-index.top-bar`, `size.panel-gap` from the edges.
- **CR-15.** No fill, edge, radius or shadow beyond Surface's glass, no hover, pressed or focus look of the pattern's own but the `brand` link's hover and pressed fills (Tokens), and no colour on any part but the `paused` part's `color.text.primary` and the out-of-date phrase's `color.text.warning`; Brand, Text and the caller's controls draw their own.

With `size='small'`, the groups are in the DOM in the order `identity`, `tools`, `filters`, the order on screen, where Mylder's composition keeps one DOM order and moves the filters to the second row with CSS `order`. The contract's checklist asks that focus move in reading order, and with one DOM order a phone's Tab would visit the filters on the second row before the tools on the first; a `size` the pattern reads can change the order in the DOM, which CSS `order` never does, with nothing changed on screen.

## Do and don't

For a caller using the pattern, with the reason.

| Do                                                                                                                              | Don't                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Set `size` to `small` under `breakpoint.phone`, in the screen's code                                                            | Expect the bar to turn into the phone's layout on its own; it holds no media query                                                             |
| Place the bar from the screen, `size.panel-gap` from the edges at `z-index.top-bar`, and wrap it in the page's `header`         | Give it a position or a `z-index` through `slotProps`, or expect it to be a landmark on its own                                                |
| Give `time` in Copenhagen's time as `HH:MM:SS`, the time the data is from, and give it again when the data changes              | Pass the time now, or a time counting up: the line says when the data is from, and the brand never claims fresher data than it has             |
| Set `outOfDate` when the app judges the data may be stale, and `paused` when the map takes no updates                           | Leave a stale time showing as if fresh, or show "Paused" by changing `updatedLabel`: the states have their own words and inks                  |
| Put a FilterChip in `filters` for each filter, with its `count`, and keep its state in the screen, such as in the address       | Put buttons, switches or a segmented control in `filters`: a filter people turn on and off is a FilterChip, and the group is named for filters |
| Put the language link, a small outline LinkButton, and the About button, a large IconButton with an 18 px-grid icon, in `tools` | Fill `tools` until the first row wraps on a phone: the brand, the status line and the tools share it                                           |
| Give `filtersLabel` the word that names what the filters hold, such as "Modes"                                                  | Leave a group of modes named "Filters" where every chip is a mode                                                                              |
| Pick `slotProps.brand.variant` `'mark'` under `breakpoint.compact-phone`                                                        | Hide the brand or its parts with `className`, which can leave a mark no screen reader hears                                                    |
| Announce a change of state, such as the data feed failing, in the screen's own polite live region                               | Make the status line a live region: its time changes on every refresh and would flood a screen reader                                          |
