# DayProfileChart

How one count moves across a day, such as the vehicles in service each hour from midnight to midnight: one line in the chart colour, the area under it, a 24-hour time axis, and a value tip that gives the time and the count under the pointer or the keys.

The design is deferred (ADR 0010, `docs/adr/0010-unattended-runs-with-design-deferred.md`): this spec decides what the drawing would, from the [contract](../components/README.md) and its Patterns section, the [foundations](../foundations/README.md), [content design](../content-design.md) and the tokens. The extracted prototype is not reachable from this branch, so nothing here rests on it. The ROADMAP names it among the patterns (7.2), and the registry files it under Data with no `dependsOn` and no `baseUiParts`, beside ReportStrip and VehicleTable, the views [DataStatePanel](data-state-panel.md) holds while their data loads. What the foundations already say of it: a chart with one series draws it in `color.chart.series`, "the vehicles in service curve", with the area under the line at `opacity.series-fill`, and its values and labels in text colours ([Semantic colours](../foundations/semantic-colours.md#charts)); its axis lines are `color.border.strong` and its axis labels `color.text.tertiary` in `typography.data-sm` ([Typography](../foundations/typography.md#roles)), which this chart's axis and scale take in `color.text.secondary` for contrast (Tokens); and its value tip is `color.surface.inverse` with `color.text.inverse` ([Elevation](../foundations/elevation.md#surfaces)).

DayProfileChart plots one series of counts across one calendar day, 00:00 to 24:00, as a line with the area under it, with no marker for the time now, no second series and no day to compare with. The one series token, `color.chart.series`, is the vehicles in service curve, and the foundations give a chart no second series colour; a marker for now, a second series or a fixed scale to compare two days can each join later as an optional prop without a break, as KioskBar leaves a date for later.

## Anatomy

Penpot page `Patterns/DayProfileChart`, not drawn yet. Family: none, since it draws no surface and sits on the panel, sheet or card that holds it, as DataStatePanel does; its value tip takes the Tooltips row's fill, text and radius. Shared parts: VisuallyHidden, for each point's words, an unknown count's "Unknown" among them. The contract's [Patterns](../components/README.md#patterns) section lets a pattern import only the helpers of `packages/perron/src/internal/nodes.ts`, so the chart composes VisuallyHidden itself where a component would take the `UnknownValue` helper (CR-5).

DayProfileChart has no variant property, so its drawing has one variant, `Default`: a whole day of hourly counts. The drawing's layers are the parts below, top to bottom: the `caption`, then the `plot` holding the `graph` (the `gridline`, `baseline`, `area` and `line` shapes), the `scale`, the `points` with one `point` each, and the `tip`, then the `axis` with its `tick`s. The `State` property it would carry is `Highlighted`, a point under the pointer or the keys, beside rest.

The axis shows five ticks, 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 and 24:00, and the scale only its top count, rounded up from the largest known count to the next multiple of half its leading power of ten (412 to 450, 1 260 to 1 500, 980 to 1 000, 8 to 8), with 0 left to the baseline. Five labels of five characters in `typography.data-sm` fit a 320 px plot with room between them; every six hours marks night, the two rush hours and the evening; "24:00" is how the 24-hour clock writes the end of a day; and one rounded figure at the top tells the eye the scale without a column of numbers the ear would hear twice, since each point already says its count.

The shapes, the scale, the axis and the tip are hidden from screen readers, and the points carry the chart to the ear: each `point` is a list item reading its time and its count, "08:00 1 260", in the day's order, and the plot's group is named by the caption, so a screen reader hears "Vehicles in service, group" and then the list. The gridline, baseline, area and line are shapes inside the one `graph` and the ticks text inside the one `axis`, drawn from `points`, so they take no `slotProps` key of their own: a caller restyles them through `className` on the root and their classes.

The chart shows a value tip for one highlighted point, under the pointer, under a finger that touches the plot, or moved along by the arrow keys once the plot has focus, with the plot one focus stop whose group points at the highlighted point through `aria-activedescendant`. [Elevation](../foundations/elevation.md#surfaces) names the chart's value tip, and [Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#rules) asks that anything shown on hover be reachable without it, so the keys and touch reach the same tip; one focus stop keeps a day of 24 to 96 points from becoming as many Tab stops; and `aria-activedescendant` on a group lets a screen reader hear each point as the keys reach it while focus stays on the plot.

DayProfileChart has no `render`, on the root or in `slotProps`, as KioskBar and StopTimeline have none: the contract's `render` is Base UI's, inherited from a Base UI props type, which a pattern cannot import, and declaring it fails the `props` check.

`points` is an array of `DayProfileChartPoint`, a type the pattern exports beside `DayProfileChartProps`:

| Field   | Type             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `time`  | `string`         | The point's time of day, 24-hour `HH:MM` from `'00:00'` to `'23:59'`, in Copenhagen's time. A point whose time doesn't read as one is left out of the drawing and the list (CR-2).                                |
| `count` | `number \| null` | Optional. How many there were at that time, a whole number from 0 up, such as `1260`. `null`, left out or not a finite number, the count is unknown: the line breaks there, and the point says "—" and "Unknown". |

A point takes its time as an `HH:MM` string and its figure as a number named `count`, which the pattern writes in digits grouped in threes by a no-break space ("1 260"), the same in every locale. `HH:MM` is how content design writes a time and how StopTimeline and KioskBar take theirs, so the chart reads no `Date` and no device time zone; `count` names what the vehicles in service curve counts without `value`, which the contract keeps for controls; a number is what a line is drawn from, where KioskBar's `figure` is a ReactNode it only shows; and `PerronProvider` gives a component its strings but no number format, so one grouping, the one KioskBar's figures show, keeps the four locales alike.

The chart takes `label` as a required `ReactNode`, shown above the plot as the figure's `figcaption`. The contract's checklist asks that everything the eye is told the ear is told, and a line with no words says nothing of what it counts; Stat names its label prop `label` the same way; and a `figcaption` names the figure natively, so the plot's group reads its name from the same element.

`className`, `style`, `ref` and the other `figure` props reach the root, as the contract's [Root](../components/README.md#root) says. `points`, `label`, `unknownLabel` and `slotProps` are taken out of the rest props first; the props type leaves out `children`, since the chart writes its own parts. No `variant`, `color` or `size`: the chart has one look, the series in the one chart colour, and it sizes to its container. No `value`, `onChange` or highlighted-point prop: the highlight is the chart's own, a passing view of one point, not a value a form or a screen holds, and a controlled highlight can join later without a break.

The chart takes Stat's `unknownLabel` name for "Unknown", as KioskBar does, so a caller learns it once.

The root is block-level and sizes to its container, as the contract asks: the chart places nothing, with no `position`, `margin` or `z-index` of its own beyond the plot's `relative`, and it keeps no width or minimum, so it fits a 320 px screen and the 376 px overview sheet alike.

The axis and the scale are `color.text.secondary`, which reaches 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes, in place of the `color.text.tertiary` the foundations give axis labels: tertiary misses 4.5:1, and `aria-hidden` does not exempt visible text from WCAG 1.4.3, so axe's `color-contrast` rule fails it in every story and in the contract's Root story. No axe rule is turned off, and tertiary stays for hints next to stronger text. The tip's `color.text.inverse` on `color.surface.inverse` is primary text on its own inverse, the pair the Tooltips row measures. The series colour carries no text, as [Semantic colours](../foundations/semantic-colours.md#charts) asks.

Nothing moves: the tip appears, follows and leaves at once, and the line redraws at once when `points` changes, with no `duration.*` token, as the contract asks of a state with no fitting duration.

## Keyboard and ARIA

No Base UI part, so nothing from Base UI here. The plot is one focus stop, and the keys move its highlight while focus stays on it.

| Key          | Does                                                                                                                                                                                       | From                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| `Tab`        | Moves focus to the plot, when `points` holds a point; the last point is highlighted and the tip shows it, unless a point is already highlighted (CR-6)                                     | The browser, then Perron |
| `ArrowRight` | Highlights the next point, staying on the last; with none highlighted, as after Escape, highlights the last point, as focus does                                                           | Perron                   |
| `ArrowLeft`  | Highlights the point before, staying on the first; with none highlighted, as after Escape, highlights the last point, as focus does                                                        | Perron                   |
| `Home`       | Highlights the first point                                                                                                                                                                 | Perron                   |
| `End`        | Highlights the last point                                                                                                                                                                  | Perron                   |
| `Escape`     | While a point is highlighted: clears the highlight and the tip, focus staying on the plot, and stops there; with none highlighted, it does nothing and goes on to whatever holds the chart | Perron                   |
| `Shift+Tab`  | Moves focus back out, to whatever came before the chart; leaving clears the highlight                                                                                                      | The browser              |

The pointer and touch reach the same tip: moving the pointer over the plot highlights the point nearest it in time, pressing or touching the plot does the same and focuses it, and the pointer leaving clears the highlight unless the plot has focus, so a tap keeps the tip until the person taps elsewhere (CR-7).

| Element                         | Role                                 | Accessible name from                                            | States announced                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

A point reads "08:00 1 260", and one with no count "05:00 Unknown". A screen reader in browse mode reads the whole list in order; one that tabs to the plot hears "Vehicles in service, group", then each point as the arrow keys reach it.

What axe won't catch, from the contract, each with how this component meets it:

- [ ] Everything the eye is told, the ear is told, for every shape each prop accepts: every point's time and count, which the line, the tip and the guide show, is heard from its list item, an unknown count as "Unknown", while the tip's "—" is `aria-hidden`; the caption, which says what the line counts, names the figure and the plot; the axis ticks and the scale's top count, eye-only context for the line's shape, are hidden, since each point says its own time and count
- [ ] Every landmark and named region has an accessible name: the figure and the plot's group are named by the caption; the chart adds no landmark
- [ ] Every default label can be overridden: `unknownLabel`
- [ ] Every shape the type accepts renders, in every variant: `label` as a string, an element and an array; `points` empty, one point, a whole day of 24, 96 points a quarter-hour apart, a part of a day, every count unknown, a lone known count between unknown ones, a count of 0, and a time that doesn't read as `HH:MM` (CR-2)
- [ ] A hidden copy kept for animation is `aria-hidden`, and a collapsed region is `inert`: none here; nothing animates or collapses. The graph, scale, tip and axis are `aria-hidden` because the points say what they show, not because they are copies
- [ ] Native semantics first: `<figure>` and `<figcaption>`, `<ol>` and `<li>` for the points, `<time>` for each point's time; `role="group"` and `aria-activedescendant` on the plot only, where no element says a focus stop that moves through its items
- [ ] Focus moves in reading order and returns on close: the plot is one stop between the caption and whatever follows; Escape clears the tip and leaves focus on the plot; nothing opens elsewhere
- [ ] Every target is at least `size.target-min` square: the one target is the whole plot, at least 107 px tall at 320 px wide; the points are not targets of their own
- [ ] Loops stop when the system asks for less motion: none here; nothing moves

## Component rules

Rules for this pattern alone, numbered so the build and the review can cite them.

- **CR-1.** The day runs from 00:00 at the plot's left edge to 24:00 at its right: a point at `HH:MM` sits at `(HH × 60 + MM) / 1440` of the plot's width, in the `graph`'s `viewBox` at that many minutes across and in `style.left` as that percentage. The plot and the axis are `direction: ltr`.
- **CR-2.** Points are drawn and listed in the order `points` gives them; the chart never sorts them. A point whose `time` doesn't read as `HH:MM` from `00:00` to `23:59` is left out of the line, the area, the list and the keys, as if it weren't given. A count is known when it is a finite number, as the `known` helper of `packages/perron/src/internal/nodes.ts` reads it; `null`, left out, `NaN` or an infinity is unknown.
- **CR-3.** The scale's top is the largest known count rounded up to the next multiple of half its leading power of ten, and never below 1: 412 is 450, 1 260 is 1 500, 980 is 1 000, 8 is 8 and 0 is 1. With no known count the scale shows nothing, the line and the area draw nothing, and the gridline, which marks the scale's top, is left out, as the `NoPoints` story shows. Every count the chart writes, in the scale, the tip and the points, is its digits grouped in threes from the right by a no-break space (U+00A0): "1 260", "450".
- **CR-4.** The line runs through the known counts in order, at `100 − count / top × 100` down the `viewBox`, and breaks at an unknown count: each run of known counts is one subpath of the line (a new `M`) and one closed shape of the area, down to the baseline at its first and last points' times. A run of one point draws a dot, through the line's round cap.
- **CR-5.** Each point's list item holds, inside VisuallyHidden, a `<time dateTime="HH:MM">` with the point's time, a space, and its count as CR-3 writes it, or, when the count is unknown, `unknownLabel` or the locale's "Unknown" as plain text: the whole item is hidden from the eye, so no "—" joins it, and the contract's Patterns section keeps a pattern from importing the `UnknownValue` helper. The list item itself draws nothing but the guide while highlighted.
- **CR-6.** The plot holds one highlighted point at most, its index in the pattern's state. On focus, if no point is highlighted, the last point is. ArrowRight and ArrowLeft move the highlight one point, stopping at the ends, and with none highlighted, as after Escape, highlight the last point, as focus does, moving one point from there on later presses; Home and End move it to the first and last, highlighted or not; each of these keys calls `preventDefault`, so the page doesn't scroll. Escape with a point highlighted clears it and calls `stopPropagation`, so the panel around the chart stays open; with none highlighted, Escape is left alone. Blur clears the highlight. While a point is highlighted, the plot's `aria-activedescendant` is its `id`, and the point carries `data-highlighted`; otherwise neither is set.
- **CR-7.** `pointermove` and `pointerdown` on the plot highlight the point nearest the pointer in time, the earlier of two equally near; `pointerleave` clears the highlight unless the plot has focus. Pressing the plot focuses it, as any `tabIndex={0}` element, and the focus handler keeps the point the pointer chose (CR-6). A finger lifting from a tap fires `pointerleave` before the browser focuses the plot, so a touch's `pointerleave` keeps the highlight and a tap keeps its tip; `pointercancel`, when a swipe scrolls the page instead, clears it unless the plot has focus. The handlers of `slotProps.plot` run first and the pattern's own after them, unless the caller's called `preventDefault`.
- **CR-8.** The tip is rendered only while a point is highlighted, along the plot's top edge, with `style.left` at the point's percentage `p` and `style.transform` `translateX(−p%)`, so it is left-aligned at 00:00, right-aligned at 24:00 and slides between, never past the plot's edges. Each tick is placed the same way, at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 per cent. The tip shows the point's time, then its count as CR-3 writes it or "—" when unknown.
- **CR-9.** Each part's props are its `slotProps` value with the pattern's own laid over them, its own last: `className` strings joined, the part's class first, through the `classes` helper, and `role`, `aria-*`, `tabIndex`, `id` and the children the pattern's. A pattern cannot import Base UI's `mergeProps`, so the merge is written in its own folder, as DataStatePanel's is. `points`, `label`, `unknownLabel` and `slotProps` are taken out of the rest props before they reach the root.
- **CR-10.** Nothing is announced on its own: no part is a live region, and a change of `points`, such as the hour's new count, redraws the chart in silence. A highlighted index past the end of new `points` moves to the last point. The chart is a view, as [DataStatePanel](data-state-panel.md) holds it: it has no loading, empty or error message of its own.
- **CR-11.** The line is `border.width.050`, and the build adds "the line of a chart" to that token's description through the `design-tokens` skill, beside the focus ring and a control's selected state, so the token's use names every reader. The plot's height is its `aspect-ratio: 3`, with no token, until a design asks for a fixed one.

## Do and don't

For a caller using the pattern, with the reason.

| Do                                                                                                                                  | Don't                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Give each point's time in Copenhagen's time as `HH:MM`, in time order, one per hour or per quarter-hour                             | Pass the device's local time, a 12-hour time or points out of order: the line runs through them as given, and the brand keeps the city's 24-hour clock |
| Give `count` `null` for an hour no data covers, so the line breaks and the point says "Unknown"                                     | Pass `0` or a guess for an hour the data doesn't know: a dip to 0 claims no vehicles ran, and the brand never claims more than the data knows          |
| Give the points so far today and let the line end at the latest                                                                     | Pad the rest of the day with zeros or the timetable's figures: the line would drop to 0 or show plans as counts                                        |
| Name what the line counts in `label`, in a few words: "Vehicles in service"                                                         | Leave the chart to a heading above it alone: the caption is what names the figure and the plot to a screen reader                                      |
| Wrap the chart in [DataStatePanel](data-state-panel.md) while its data loads, is empty or could not be loaded                       | Show an empty chart, or one of all-unknown points, while the data is on its way: the baseline alone reads as a day with nothing in it                  |
| Let the chart redraw in silence as each hour's count arrives, and announce a change of state in the screen's own polite live region | Make the chart or its tip a live region: every new count would be read out                                                                             |
| Give the chart its container's width, from a 320 px phone to the overview sheet, and let its height follow                          | Set a fixed height or width through `className`: the plot keeps its shape by its ratio, and a squeezed one distorts the curve                          |
