# SourceLine

One caption line that names where a panel's data comes from and the time of that data, "Rejseplanen · updated 08:14:05", with an optional second source and its own time, and two states: paused, and may be out of date. It never counts up, and it never blends two sources into one "last updated".

Its sources are the [contract](../components/README.md) and its Patterns section, the [foundations](../foundations/README.md) ("Honest. ## Anatomy

Penpot page `Patterns/SourceLine`. Family: none: it is a line of supporting text on its container's surface, not a row, field, popup, panel or tooltip, and it draws no surface of its own (CR-9). Shared parts: none: every word is said in text, and nothing is told by an icon or a shape.

The root is a Text in `typography.caption` at `emphasis='secondary'`, rendered as a `<p>`, holding the sources inline in reading order. Typography gives `typography.caption` to "notes, second lines in rows … map credits", and the contract's Families give a row's second line `typography.caption` in `color.text.secondary`, which is what a source line under a panel's content is; secondary, not tertiary, because Rejseplanen's terms ask that the line be read, and tertiary misses 4.5:1 on purpose (Attribution's credits take secondary for the same reason); Text is the entry in `dependsOn` and sets the role, the colour, `margin: 0` and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` itself (its CR-6 and CR-11); and a `<p>` is a paragraph of the panel, read in place, where a `<ul>` would announce "list, 1 item" for the common one-source line.

The line reads, in order: the paused word when paused, then each source as its name, a "·", and its time in one owned phrase, "updated 08:14:05", or, when that source may be out of date, "updated 08:09:40, may be out of date"; two sources are joined by a "·". Each phrase is one owned string with a `{time}` placeholder, filled by `interpolate` from `packages/perron/src/internal/strings.ts`, as the contract's [Words](../components/README.md#words) asks of a string that wraps the caller's words, so each locale puts the time where its grammar needs it. So "Rejseplanen · updated 08:14:05", "Paused · Rejseplanen · updated 08:14:05" and "Rejseplanen · updated 08:14:05 · Vehicle positions · updated 08:09:40, may be out of date".

The drawing will have a `root` board holding, in order, a `paused` text layer, a `separator`, and one or two `source` boards, each holding `name`, `separator` and `time` text layers, with a `separator` between two sources. It has no variant property: paused and out of date come from the data, as ReportStrip's unknown counts do, so its one variant is `Default`, and no `State` property, since nothing in it is interactive and its one design state is rest; paused is rest with `data-paused`, and a source that may be out of date is rest with `data-out-of-date`.

The "·" is text the ear reads as the screen reader reads it, not `aria-hidden`, so what the eye is told is what the ear is told.

A caller who needs the line inside another element wraps it.

## Props

In MUI's layout. Every prop here gets this description as its JSDoc. Slots come last.

The line takes its sources as one required `sources` array of one or two `SourceLineSource` objects, `{ name, time, outOfDate? }`, and its paused state as a `paused` boolean. A pattern takes Mylder's data and lays it out, as ReportStrip takes `counts` and VehicleTable `vehicles`, so every panel writes its line alike, in the same words and order, which is what the ticket asks; a tuple type of one or two entries says "an optional second source" in the type itself, where a `secondSource` and `secondTime` beside `source` and `time` would be four props for one idea; out of date belongs to a source, not to the line, since a stale board under a fresh snapshot is the case the ticket names, so it is a field of each source; paused belongs to the line, since a paused panel takes no update from any source; and both are adjectives, `false` by default, as the contract's booleans are.

`sources` holds `SourceLineSource` objects, a type the pattern exports beside `SourceLineProps`:

| Field       | Type      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `name`      | `string`  | Required. The source's name, as its terms ask it to be named, such as `'Rejseplanen'`                                                                                                                                                                                  |

| `outOfDate` | `boolean` | Optional, `false` when left out. The app judges this source's data may be out of date, such as when its last refresh failed or its time is past the app's limit; the line says "updated 08:09:40, may be out of date" for it, and never judges it from the time (CR-6) |

Each source's `time` is required, and a time is never unknown. A source line names data the panel shows, and data the panel shows came at a time; a panel with no data yet is DataStatePanel's loading or error status, not a line with "—", and a time that says nothing would claim a source the panel doesn't have. ReportStrip's counts can be unknown because a state can go uncounted; a source the line names always has a time, so the `known` helper and `UnknownValue` stay out.

`className`, `style`, `ref`, `id`, `aria-*`, `data-*` and the other `p` props reach the root, as the contract's [Root](../components/README.md#root) says. `sources`, `paused`, the three `*Label` props and `slotProps` are taken out of the rest props first; the props type leaves out `children`, since the line writes its own parts, and `color`, which Text declares as its intent (CR-10). No `variant`, `color` or `size`: the line has one look, Text's caption, and a second can join later as an optional prop without a break.

SourceLine takes a `slotProps` key for each part but the root, each value given to every copy of its part, as ReportStrip does: the skill that writes this spec asks for one per part, and a key per source would be ten keys for two copies of one part.

## Tokens

One row per part, state and property. Nothing in the line is interactive, so every row is rest; paused is rest with `data-paused` on the root, and a source that may be out of date is rest with `data-out-of-date` on its `source`. Nothing is pressed, so there are no hover, pressed or cursor rows. Text keeps its own tokens, read from [its spec](../components/text.md#tokens) and not set again here: the root's `typography.caption` with its tracking, its `color.text.secondary`, its `margin: 0` and its `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.

| Part                                          | State | Property        | Token                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `root`                                        | rest  | `font`          | none: Text's `typography.caption`, set by `variant='caption'`; SourceLine sets nothing here                     |
| `root`                                        | rest  | `color`         | none: Text's `color.text.secondary`, set by `emphasis='secondary'`; SourceLine sets nothing here                |
| `root`                                        | rest  | `overflow-wrap` | none: Text's `anywhere`, the long-word rule, so a source's long name breaks inside itself in a narrow container |
| `paused`                                      | rest  | `color`         | `color.text.primary`                                                                                            |
| `name`, `separator`                           | rest  | `color`, `font` | none: inherited from the root                                                                                   |
| `time`                                        | rest  | `font`          | none: inherited, the root's `typography.caption`                                                                |
| `time`                                        | rest  | `color`         | none: inherited, the root's `color.text.secondary`                                                              |
| `time`, in a `source` with `data-out-of-date` | rest  | `color`         | `color.text.warning`                                                                                            |

Each time is set in the line's own `typography.caption`, in its proportional figures, inside its owned phrase. The phrase is one owned string with the time in it, so the time is not an element of its own that a second role could reach; one face for the whole line keeps it calm, where JetBrains Mono beside the caption read heavier and wider than the words around it; and the caption keeps its proportional figures, since Schibsted Grotesk's tabular figures, `tabular-nums`, set the colons of "08:14:05" and the comma after it on figure-wide advances, which spread the time apart ("08 : 14 : 05 ,"), and the few pixels the line shifts as the seconds change sit at its end or after a "·". A time's digits and colons hold no break opportunity, so a time never breaks over two lines; the line wraps at the spaces of the phrase and after each "·".

"Paused" is in `color.text.primary`, and the phrase of a source that may be out of date, "updated 08:09:40, may be out of date", in `color.text.warning`, with the rest of the line in `color.text.secondary`. A paused line and a doubtful source are what the reader must not miss, so each takes a step past the line's secondary ink: paused is the viewer's or the app's choice, not a fault, so it takes the neutral full-strength ink, 11.21:1 or more on every surface; may be out of date is a doubt about the data, which the warning intent says, at 4.78:1 or more on every surface ([Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#text-on-surfaces)); and both are words first, so the colour is never the only sign. No icon: the line is one caption run, and no icon in `packages/perron/icons/` means paused.

The line draws no surface and places nothing: no `background`, `border`, `radius`, `shadow`, `position`, `inset`, `z-index` or outer `margin` (CR-9). It sits on the surface of the panel, card or glass that holds it.

In forced colours every word takes the system's text colour on its own, and paused and out of date are said in words, so the line adds no forced-colours rule.

## Owned strings

Three new keys, each in `packages/perron/src/locale/en.ts` and the Danish, Swedish and Norwegian dictionaries, which agents write. All three are named for the source line, since each is worded for it alone: a status line elsewhere, such as the TopBar's "Updated 08:14:05", needs a sentence-case "Updated" under a key of its own. The two phrases wrap the caller's time, so each is one string with a `{time}` placeholder, filled by `interpolate`, and its override takes the same placeholder (the contract's [Words](../components/README.md#words)): Danish, Swedish and Norwegian put the time where their grammar needs it. Both phrases start in lower case because each sits inside the line, after a "·", as "min" and "to" sit inside theirs; "Paused" starts the line, in sentence case. The names are the caller's, the times are figures, and the "·" holds no letter, which `expectOwnedStrings` passes.

| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sourceUpdated`   | updated {time}                     | `sourceUpdatedLabel`   | Each `time` part of a source that is not out of date, seen and read in place  |
| `sourcePaused`    | Paused                             | `sourcePausedLabel`    | The `paused` part, first in a paused line, seen and read in place             |
| `sourceOutOfDate` | updated {time}, may be out of date | `sourceOutOfDateLabel` | Each `time` part of a source whose `outOfDate` is set, seen and read in place |

## Component rules

Rules for this pattern alone, numbered so the build and the review can cite them.

- **CR-1.** The root is `<Text variant="caption" emphasis="secondary" render={<p />}>`, holding, in order: the `paused` part and a `separator` when `paused` is set; then each source of `sources`, in the caller's order, with a `separator` between two. Each `separator` has a no-break space (U+00A0) before it and a space after it, outside it, as text, so the line wraps after a "·" and never starts a line with one. Nothing else renders inside the root.
- **CR-2.** Each `source` holds, in order, its `name`, a no-break space, a `separator`, a space and its `time` part, so the line reads "Rejseplanen · updated 08:14:05". The time part's phrase is one owned string filled by `interpolate`, never an owned word joined to the caller's time in code (the contract's [Words](../components/README.md#words)); the name is a text run of its own.
- **CR-3.** Each `time` is `<time dateTime={time}>`, holding its phrase with `{time}` filled with the caller's string as it is. The pattern runs no timer, reads no clock and formats nothing: no `Date`, no "8 s ago", no countdown, no count up from the time. A time changes only when the caller gives a new one. KioskBar's clock and VehicleTable's Last seen take their times the same way.
- **CR-4.** Every source keeps its own name and its own phrase and time. The line never joins two sources under one "updated", never shows one time for both, and never picks the older or the newer one to stand for the line.
- **CR-5.** When `paused` is set, the root carries the bare `data-paused` attribute and the line starts with the `paused` part, the owned string "Paused" or `sourcePausedLabel`, then a `separator`; each source still shows its own time, the time of the data the panel shows. With `paused` off, no `paused` part and no `data-paused` render.
- **CR-6.** When a source's `outOfDate` is set, that `source` carries the bare `data-out-of-date` attribute, and its `time` part holds the owned phrase `sourceOutOfDate`, "updated {time}, may be out of date", or `sourceOutOfDateLabel`, in `color.text.warning`, in place of `sourceUpdated`. The pattern never decides this itself from a time: the app does, as [Data states](../foundations/data-states.md#thresholds) says of a vehicle's state. A source given no `outOfDate` or `false` shows none of it.
- **CR-7.** The pattern reads its words with `useOwnedStrings({ sourceUpdated: sourceUpdatedLabel, sourcePaused: sourcePausedLabel, sourceOutOfDate: sourceOutOfDateLabel })` from `packages/perron/src/PerronProvider.tsx`, and fills each phrase's `{time}` with `interpolate`.
- **CR-8.** The line is never interactive: no hover, focus, pressed or selected look, no tab stop, no `tabIndex`, no handler of its own. A source the rider can follow to its terms is a link in Attribution or the screen's about page, not in the line.
- **CR-9.** The line draws no surface and places nothing: no `background`, `border`, `radius`, `shadow`, `position`, `inset`, `z-index` or outer `margin`. It sits on the surface of whatever holds it, and its screen places it.
- **CR-10.** The props type extends `Omit<ComponentPropsWithRef<'p'>, 'children' | 'color'>` and declares `sources`, `paused`, the three `*Label` props and `slotProps`, each taken out of the rest props before the spread, so none reaches the DOM. The root spreads the rest props onto Text, then sets its own `variant`, `emphasis`, `render`, `ref`, `data-paused` and class, `pn-SourceLine` then the caller's `className` by the `classes` helper from `packages/perron/src/internal/nodes.ts`, so its own win, and the root's class reads `pn-Text pn-SourceLine`, then the caller's.
- **CR-11.** Each inner part's props are its `slotProps` value with the pattern's own laid over it: the class joins the part's own first, then the caller's, by `classes`, and the children, `dateTime` and `data-out-of-date` are the pattern's. A pattern cannot import Base UI's `mergeProps`, so the merge is a spread, then the pattern's own props, as ReportStrip's CR-12 writes it.
- **CR-12.** The line is no live region: neither the root nor any part sets `aria-live`, `role="status"` or `role="log"`. A screen that must announce a change of state does it in its own polite live region.
- **CR-13.** At 320 px wide, in every locale, the line wraps at its spaces, never before a "·", and never overflows its container: a time's digits never break, since digits and colons hold no break opportunity, and a name longer than the line breaks inside itself by Text's `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
- **CR-14.** The pattern imports only Text, `PerronProvider`, the `classes` and `interpolate` helpers, React and its own CSS, as the contract's Patterns section says; never `@base-ui/react`.

## Do and don't

For a caller using the pattern, with the reason.

| Do                                                                                                                    | Don't                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Put a SourceLine on every panel that shows a source's data, in the same place, so the reader finds it on every screen | Leave the line off a panel because another panel on the screen has one: Rejseplanen's terms ask for it on every screen   |
| Give each source its own entry with the time of its own data                                                          | Pass one source named "Rejseplanen and positions" with one time: a stale feed would read as fresh under the other's time |
| Give the time of the data the panel shows, and give it again when that data changes                                   | Give the time the screen last rendered, or the device's clock: the line would say the data is newer than it is           |
| Set `outOfDate` from the app's own judgement, such as a failed refresh or a time past its limit                       | Leave a source that failed to refresh without `outOfDate`: its time alone doesn't tell the reader it stopped             |
| Set `paused` while the panel takes no updates, and leave each time as it was                                          | Count the time up, or show "8 s ago", while paused: the line would claim a freshness the panel doesn't have              |
| Announce a change of state, such as a source going out of date, in the screen's own polite live region                | Make the line a live region: its times change every few seconds and would flood a screen reader                          |
| Credit a map's sources with [Attribution](../components/attribution.md), with links to their terms                    | Use the line for the map's credits: it names the data a panel shows and when, not the map's tiles                        |
