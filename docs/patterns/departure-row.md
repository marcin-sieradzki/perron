# DepartureRow

One departure from a stop, as one row of the stop's list of departures: the line's badge and where it goes, how fresh the vehicle's data is, and how soon it leaves, with its timetabled time and how late it runs.

<!-- run notes -->

The design is deferred under ADR 0010 (`docs/adr/0010-unattended-runs-with-design-deferred.md`), so there is no read-back, code map or export: this spec decides the variant property, the layers, the design states and the token for every part. Its sources are the [contract](../components/README.md) and its Patterns section, the [foundations](../foundations/README.md), [Data states](../foundations/data-states.md), [Typography](../foundations/typography.md#roles), [content design](../content-design.md), [Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md), `list_tokens`, and the specs of the three components in `dependsOn`, [LineBadge](../components/line-badge.md), [Delay](../components/delay.md) and [VehicleDot](../components/vehicle-dot.md). The ROADMAP names it first in 7.2, Patterns, and no more. The extracted prototype is not reachable from this branch, so nothing here rests on it. It follows the merged patterns' precedents: [DetailHeader](detail-header.md)'s `line` and `lineName`, [StopTimeline](stop-timeline.md)'s `time` and `delay` and its Delay label props, and [DisruptionCard](disruption-card.md)'s `slotProps` keyed by the pattern's own parts. No sibling is in flight with a spec: VehicleDotLabel and VehicleDotLegend are on their branches with their design deferred and no spec yet.

## Anatomy

Penpot page `Patterns/DepartureRow`. Family: none, since the row is not pressed and draws no fill, border or radius of its own; its text follows the Rows family's text, a name in `typography.label` and its second line in `typography.caption` with `color.text.secondary`. Shared parts: `VisuallyHidden`, through the `UnknownValue` helper, for a countdown no one knows.

[PENDING] Is the row drawn as a grid of three columns and two lines: the line's `badge` at the start, centred on both lines; the `destination` over the `status`, the vehicle's dot and its state's word, in the middle; and the `countdown` over the `schedule`, the timetabled `time` and the `delay`, at the end, with no platform, no tag, no action and no "Show on map" of its own? Recommended: yes: the line, the destination and how soon it leaves are what a rider scans a stop's departures for, so they sit on the first line, the start and the end; [Data states](../foundations/data-states.md) asks that a vehicle's state be written in words next to its dot, and [Typography](../foundations/typography.md#roles) gives the countdown `typography.data-lg` and "the time under a countdown" `typography.data-sm`, so the second line holds the state's word under the destination and the time under the countdown; a platform, a tag such as "Night bus" and an action are what no source asks of the row yet, and each can join later as an optional prop without a break.

The drawing is a `root` board holding eight layers in reading order: `badge`, `destination`, `status` (holding `dot` and the state's word), `countdown`, and `schedule` (holding `time` and `delay`).

## Props

Its props type is the `<div>`'s props, `ComponentPropsWithRef<'div'>`, without `children`, with its own members added; the `<div>`'s other attributes reach the root and get no row, as in MUI's tables. ## Tokens

One row per part, state and property. DepartureRow is not interactive, so its one design state is rest (CR-8); the `variant` values change only the composed components and the words. LineBadge, VehicleDot and Delay keep their own tokens, as their specs give them; rows marked "through" are theirs, listed so the drawing has them.

[PENDING] Does the row space its parts with `space.300` between its three columns, `space.050` between its two lines, `space.200` above and below its content, `space.100` between the dot and its word and between the time and the delay, and no divider, fill or radius of its own? | `countdown`   | none: a generic `<span>`         | Its text: the figure and "min", or "Now"; the "~" is `aria-hidden`; an unknown countdown's "—" is `aria-hidden`, and "Unknown" is read | none             |
| `time`        | `time`, from the native `<time>` | Its text, "08:14", with `dateTime` set to the same `HH:MM`                                                                             | none             |
| `delay`       | Delay's own: generic text        | Delay's own words: "3 min late", "On time", "Delay unknown"                                                                            | none             |

A live departure reads "5C, Husum Torv, Live, 4 min, 08:14, On time", and an estimated one "5C, Husum Torv, Estimated, 6 min, 08:16, 3 min late": the word "Estimated" tells the ear what the "~" tells the eye.

What axe won't catch, from the contract, each with how this component meets it:

- [ ] Everything the eye is told, the ear is told, for every shape each prop accepts: the line's colour is named by `lineName`, read from the badge; the dot's shape by the state's word beside it, read once, since the dot is `aria-hidden` (CR-6); the "~" by that word, "Estimated", "Timetable only" or "No signal" (CR-4); the countdown by its figure and "min", or "Now"; an unknown countdown by "Unknown" (CR-3); the delay by Delay's own words, and a timetable-only or no-signal vehicle's "—" by "Delay unknown" (CR-5).
- [ ] Every landmark and named region has an accessible name: none here; the row is no region, and a list of rows is the caller's.
- [ ] Every default label can be overridden: `nowLabel`, `unknownLabel`, the five state labels from `liveLabel` to `noSignalLabel`, and Delay's five, `minutesUnitLabel`, `minutesLateLabel`, `minutesEarlyLabel`, `onTimeLabel` and `delayUnknownLabel`.
- [ ] Every shape the type accepts renders, in every variant: `lineName` and `destination` render a string, an element and a list alike; `countdown` positive, under 45 seconds, zero, negative, fractional (rounded) and not finite (CR-3); `time` given and left out; `delay` a number, `null` and left out, in every `variant`; a long destination wraps under itself (CR-9).
- [ ] A hidden copy kept for animation is `aria-hidden`, and a collapsed region is `inert`: none here; nothing animates or collapses. The `dot` and the "~" are `aria-hidden` because a word says them, not for animation.
- [ ] Native semantics first: plain `div`s and `span`s, a `<time>` for the time, and the composed components' own; no ARIA but `aria-hidden` on the dot, the "~" and the unknown "—".
- [ ] Focus moves in reading order and returns on close: the row takes no focus; its DOM order, badge, destination, state, countdown, time and delay, is its reading order on screen.
- [ ] Every target is at least `size.target-min` square: no target; the row is not pressed (CR-8).
- [ ] Loops stop when the system asks for less motion: none here; nothing moves. A countdown that changes is new props from the screen, not an animation.

## Component rules

Rules for this pattern alone, numbered so the build and the review can cite them.

- **CR-1.** The pattern imports only `LineBadge`, `VehicleDot` and `Delay`, the `UnknownValue` helper and `classes` and `given` from `packages/perron/src/internal/`, `useOwnedStrings` from `PerronProvider`, React and its own CSS, as the contract's [Patterns](../components/README.md#patterns) section says; never `@base-ui/react`. The types of `line`, `variant` and the `slotProps` come through type-only imports of `LineBadgeProps`, `VehicleDotProps` and `DelayProps`.
- **CR-2.** The root is a `<div>` with `className` `pn-DepartureRow` then the caller's, and always carries `data-variant`, the default `live` included. The caller's rest props are spread on the root and the row's own class and data attribute after them, so the row's win; every prop the props type declares, `variant`, `line`, `lineName`, `destination`, `countdown`, `time`, `delay`, every `*Label` prop and `slotProps`, is taken out of the rest, so none reaches the DOM.
- **CR-3.** The countdown comes from `countdown` alone. A number that isn't finite (`NaN`, `Infinity`) shows `UnknownValue` with the `unknown` word: "—" for the eye, "Unknown" for the ear. Under 45 seconds, zero and negative included, it shows the `now` word. Otherwise it shows the whole minutes and, after a text space, the `minutesUnit` word, the number and the word in separate text runs: "4 min". It never shows seconds or a clock time in place of the minutes.

  [PENDING] Are the whole minutes `Math.round(countdown / 60)`, so 89 seconds show "1 min" and 90 seconds "2 min", with no upper bound past which the row shows a clock time instead? Recommended: yes: Delay rounds its minutes with `Math.round`, so the countdown and the delay round alike; from 45 seconds, where "Now" ends, rounding gives "1 min" with no gap; and a departure an hour off still reads as minutes, with its timetabled time under it, so the row needs no second format.

- **CR-4.** [PENDING] Does a "~" come straight before the countdown's figure when `variant` is `estimated`, `timetable-only` or `no-signal`, in its own `aria-hidden` span, and never before "Now", an unknown countdown, or a `live` or `ageing` one? Recommended: yes: content design asks for "~" before estimated times; a vehicle placed from the timetable and the latest delay, one with no real-time data and one silent for over 5 minutes all leave at a time worked out rather than reported, while a live or ageing report places the vehicle itself; the state's word beside the dot says the same to the ear, so the "~" needs no word of its own; and "Now" and "—" are no time to qualify.
- **CR-5.** A `timetable-only` or `no-signal` row always renders its Delay with `minutes={null}`, whatever `delay` holds and when `delay` is left out, as [Data states](../foundations/data-states.md#delays) says those states always show an unknown delay. Any other row renders its Delay with `minutes={delay}` only when `delay` is given, `null` included; left out, it renders no Delay.
- **CR-6.** The `status` part shows the `dot`, a VehicleDot with the row's `variant` and `line`, `size='small'`, `aria-hidden="true"` and the five state labels, then the state's owned word, with its `*Label` override laid over it. The word is visible text, so a screen reader hears the state once, from the word, as VehicleDot's CR-6 asks of a caller that writes the word beside the dot.
- **CR-7.** Each inner part's props are its `slotProps` value with the row's own laid over it: its `className` joins after the part's class (`pn-DepartureRow-<part>`, then the caller's), and its other own props (`line`, `size`, `variant`, `aria-hidden`, `minutes`, `dateTime`, the labels and the children) win. The pattern joins them by hand, with `classes`, since it can't import `mergeProps`.
- **CR-8.** The row is text: no part is a link or a button, it has no hover, pressed, focus or selected look, no tab stop and no role. A screen that opens a departure puts a Perron link or button beside the list, or makes its own list item the link, and the row has no pointer feedback to draw. DepartureRow is not a live region either: a screen that updates its departures announces a change of state, such as a departure leaving or turning to no signal, in its own polite live region, never every minute's countdown ([Accessibility](../foundations/accessibility.md#what-a-person-checks)).
- **CR-9.** The row sizes to its container, with no width of its own. The middle column is `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so the destination and the state's word wrap long words (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) within it at 320 px, while the badge, the countdown and the schedule never wrap or shrink.
- **CR-10.** The `time` part renders as `<time dateTime={time}>{time}</time>`, the `HH:MM` given. The `schedule` part renders only when it holds a `time` or a Delay (CR-5); with neither, the countdown stands alone at the end and the second line holds the state's word only.
- **CR-11.** The stories use only lines whose badge pairs reach 4.5:1 (M2, M3, E, F, H, bus and regional), so axe's `color-contrast` rule stays on in every story; M1, M4, A, B and C miss it by rule, as LineBadge's CR-6 says.
- **CR-12.** The `delay` part's font and tracking are `typography.data-sm`, the time's, set in a rule whose selector outranks `.pn-Delay`'s own, such as `.pn-Delay.pn-DepartureRow-delay`, so the order the two stylesheets load in can't undo it; Delay keeps its colour, its `tabular-nums` and its `nowrap`.

## Do and don't

For a caller using the pattern, with the reason.

| Do                                                                                                                                | Don't                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Work out `variant` from the departing vehicle's last report, with `vehicle-dot.live-threshold` and `vehicle-dot.ageing-threshold` | Pass `live` for a departure no vehicle reports on yet: the row would claim more than the data knows; pass `timetable-only` |
| Give `countdown` as the seconds to the expected departure, worked out from the screen's clock, and update it as the clock moves   | Pass the timetabled seconds for a late departure: the countdown and the delay would disagree                               |
| Give the timetabled `time` and let the Delay say how late it runs                                                                 | Pass the expected time as `time` as well as a delay: the rider adds the delay twice                                        |
| Set a stop's departures as a `<ul>`, each row in its own `<li>`, under a Section that names the stop or the platform              | Put a list of rows in a live region: every countdown's change would be read                                                |
| Keep `lineName` the short name riders know and `destination` the place: "5C" towards "Husum Torv"                                 | Put the line in `destination`, "5C Husum Torv": the badge already reads "5C" first                                         |
| Open a departure from a Perron link or button the screen sets beside the list, or from the list item made a link                  | Add `onClick` or `tabIndex` to the row: it has no focus ring, role or pressed look                                         |
