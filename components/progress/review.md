## P2

- **P2-1.** `packages/perron/src/components/Progress/Progress.css:65` The `width` transition starts the moment `data-progressing` replaces `data-indeterminate`, so when a task's first figure arrives the fill eases from the sweeping segment's `40%` instead of appearing at the figure. In the Indeterminate story with `value` changed from `null` to `5`, the fill jumped to the inline start at 146 px (40% of the 364 px track) and shrank to 18 px over 800 ms, while the figure and `aria-valuetext` already said "5%". On the commonest change a progress bar makes, from not known to known, the bar runs backwards and the eye reads "40%" as the ear hears "5%". This breaks the reason the spec gives for the 30% rest at `docs/components/progress.md:104` (the segment must never read as a share done from the inline start) and the checklist line "Everything the eye is told, the ear is told". One fix is to size the segment with `inset-inline-end` in the `pn-Progress-sweep` keyframes and leave `width` auto, so `width` has nothing to ease from. CR-6 at `docs/components/progress.md:162` should then say that a value arriving after the indeterminate status does not animate (Polish).

## P3

- **P3-1.** `packages/perron/src/components/Progress/Progress.css:73` The sweep runs on `easing.standard`, which slows the segment as it slides out past the inline end. The round 2 render of the Indeterminate story, in both themes, caught it there, with the segment at 379 px on a track that ends at 380 px and the track empty. This works against the spec's own reason for the loop at `docs/components/progress.md:103`: "a still bar reads as stuck or empty". The maintainer may prefer the `linear` keyword or an easing token built for loops, or may keep the pause on purpose (Direction).
- **P3-2.** `packages/perron/src/components/Progress/Progress.css:71` Outside reduced motion, `.pn-Progress-indicator[data-indeterminate]` gives the segment no resting `inset-inline-start`. Wherever the animation does not run, such as a Playwright screenshot with `animations: 'disabled'`, the segment falls back to the inline start and reads as "40% done". The spec's 30% rest is there to stop exactly that reading (CR-6). Also, `scripts/visual.ts:224` takes its screenshots with animations running, so once the deferred design lands, the Indeterminate pixel diff will depend on where the sweep happens to be. Setting `inset-inline-start: 30%` on the base rule (the running animation overrides it), and emulating reduced motion in the visual stage, would fix both (Direction).
- **P3-3.** `packages/perron/src/locale/da.ts:43` The build agent wrote the Danish "I gang" for `inProgress`, and "Pågår" for the Swedish and Norwegian (`packages/perron/src/locale/sv.ts:43`, `packages/perron/src/locale/nb.ts:43`). The word is not in content design's fixed table of data-state words, so a native reader should confirm it reads naturally when heard after a task's name, as in "Eksporterer rapporten, I gang" (content-audit step 2).
