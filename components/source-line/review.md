## P2

- **P2-1.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:167` CR-2 has the line build each source by joining owned words to the caller's in code: "updated", a space, then the time (`packages/perron/src/patterns/SourceLine/SourceLine.tsx:176-189`), and the time, `', '`, then "may be out of date" (`SourceLine.tsx:193-199`). Each owned word is kept "a text run of its own". That breaks the contract's Words rule: a string that wraps the caller's words, such as "From {time}", is one owned string with a `{time}` placeholder filled by `interpolate`, and "is never built by joining an owned word to the caller's in code". The rule came in with the merge from main, and siblings already follow it: DepartureRow's `expectedTime: 'Expected {time}'`, and StopTimeline's `fromTime` and `untilTime`. The contract wins over the spec, so CR-2, the Anatomy's `updated` and `outOfDate` rows (lines 26 and 28), the Owned strings table (line 158) and the four dictionaries need the time as a placeholder, for example `sourceUpdated: 'updated {time}'`, so that each locale places the time and its own words where its grammar needs them. The Danish `'er måske forældet'` already has to add a verb to fit after the comma. Each `*Label` override then takes the same placeholders.
- **P2-2.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:191` The check this round added to `Narrow`, that no line starts with a "·" (`packages/perron/src/patterns/SourceLine/SourceLine.stories.tsx:121-124`, run at lines 490 and 504), proves nothing at the width it runs at, so CR-13's "never before a '·'" (line 178) and CR-1's no-break space have no test (rubric step 7). I rendered the story with each U+00A0 turned back into a plain space. At 320 px, no line started with a "·" in any of the four locales: en and sv broke at "Vehicle | positions", and da and nb broke before "Vehicle" and inside the last words. Removing the fix therefore keeps the test green. In Swedish with plain spaces, a line does start with a "·" at 280 to 284 px and at 382 to 388 px; with the no-break spaces it never does at any width I tried. The step needs a container width, or a sweep of widths, where a plain space would put a "·" at the start of a line.

## P3

- **P3-1.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:154` The new reason for scoping `sourceUpdated` and `sourcePaused` ("each is worded for it alone") fits `mayBeOutOfDate` just as well: it is lower case and sits after a comma in this line only. Yet that key keeps a bare name, so the three keys of one component, and their overrides `sourceUpdatedLabel`, `sourcePausedLabel` and `mayBeOutOfDateLabel`, are named two ways. The maintainer may want one rule before the keys ship, perhaps when P2-1 reworks them.
- **P3-2.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:105` The `time` row's `white-space` reason says "the line wraps at the spaces around it", and the CSS comment repeats it (`packages/perron/src/patterns/SourceLine/SourceLine.css:13-14`). A time followed by a separator now has a no-break space after it, so the line can no longer wrap there. Those lines could say the line wraps after the "·" that follows a time, as CR-1 and CR-13 now do.
- **P3-3.** `patterns-sourceline--narrow light and dark, name` At 320 px, the Swedish line (and the English one) wraps inside the source name, "Vehicle | positions", so the second line starts with the last word of a name. CR-13 allows this, since it only says "wraps at its spaces". A no-break space inside a name is the caller's choice, but the maintainer may want names kept whole where they fit. This was missed in round 1.
