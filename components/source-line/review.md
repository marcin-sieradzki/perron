## P2

- **P2-1.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:100` This round rewrote the line that moves the time out of `typography.data`, and it rests on a false premise. It says "the phrase is one owned string with the time in it, so the time is not an element of its own that a second role could reach". But `interpolate`, which CR-2 and CR-7 name, takes elements as values. The contract's Shared helpers table says it fills placeholders "with the caller's words or elements", and its JSDoc says "with an element among them, an array of the template's words and the values" (`packages/perron/src/internal/strings.ts:11-15`). So `interpolate(strings.sourceUpdated, { time: <time …>{time}</time> })` would keep one owned string under the Words rule and still give the time an element that a role can reach. The same line also says that the pixels the line shifts as the seconds change "sit at its end or after a '·'". In the out-of-date phrase, though, ", may be out of date" follows the time inside the phrase and shifts with it, and a locale is free to put words after `{time}` in either phrase. The contract's helper description holds over the spec. The line needs a true reason for the caption face (the tabular-colon spread it gives is true: I measured a colon at 3.38 px proportional and 7.63 px tabular, and "08:14:05," at 49.8 px and 68.6 px), or the time goes back into its own element in the data role, as brief answer 5 had it.
- **P2-2.** `packages/perron/src/patterns/SourceLine/SourceLine.tsx:24` This round changed the `outOfDate` row of the spec's `SourceLineSource` table (`docs/patterns/source-line.md:77`) to "the line says "updated 08:09:40, may be out of date" for it", but the field's JSDoc still reads "the line adds "may be out of date" after its time". The Props section says "Every prop here gets this description as its JSDoc" (line 51), and the field table sits in that section, so the spec row holds and the JSDoc should be its text. This is the one row of the Props or field tables whose JSDoc this round's fix did not carry over.

- **P3-2.** `docs/patterns/source-line.md:167` CR-13 and line 100 now say a time "never" breaks because "digits and colons hold no break opportunity". With `white-space: nowrap` removed, the time inherits Text's `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, which breaks inside a word that is wider than its line. So the claim holds at 320 px, where a time is about 50 px wide, but it is not absolute. "Never breaks at any width the line supports" would say what is true.
