## P2

- **P2-1.** `docs/components/accordion.md:201` The Stories table's `play` tests leave these lines of the coverage checklist unticked, which the contract's Stories rule ("a `play` test for every behaviour the component promises") asks for: (1) the Keyboard table's `Tab` row says Tab moves "into an open panel's first focusable element", but no story's panel holds a focusable element and no play tabs into one; (2) CR-13 puts the hairline on an item after another item only, never on the outer edges, but no play reads `border-block-start` on the first and later items; (3) CR-12 says hover, pressed, focus, open and disabled leave the trigger's and title's boxes unchanged, but no play compares them with the rest boxes; (4) CR-8 says neither hover nor pressed applies to a disabled trigger, but `ItemDisabled` never checks `stateFill(button)` or `stateFill(button, ':active')` on the disabled trigger; (5) CR-14 gives a disabled trigger and its chevron `GrayText` under forced colours, and no play reads that rule; (6) the Base UI behaviour the spec keeps, `keepMounted` on the root or through `slotProps.panel` holding a closed panel `hidden` and out of the tree (CR-1, CR-9's no-`display` rule, Props line 66 and line 83), has no story; (7) the Base UI behaviour the `disabled` row keeps, a disabled root making every trigger `disabled` (line 64), has no story; (8) CR-5 says `slotProps.trigger` and `slotProps.panel` reach their parts with each part's class kept, but only `slotProps.header` is tested (`HeadingLevel`).
- **P2-2.** `docs/components/accordion.md:228` The Do line says to set the heading level with `slotProps.header.render` when the accordion sits "directly under a dialog's `h2`". That contradicts line 54, which makes `h3` the default because an accordion sits under a panel's `h2`. Dialog's title is an `h2` too (dialog.md line 24), so the default `h3` is already the right level there and the advice would make the outline skip a level or repeat one. Line 54 holds, and the Do line should name only the case of an accordion under an `h3`.

## P3

- **P3-1.** `docs/components/accordion.md:165` The contract's checklist asks that "a collapsed region is `inert`". The spec meets it with unmounting, `hidden` or `hidden="until-found"`, never `inert`. Those do keep a closed panel out of the tree and the Tab order. While a panel closes, though, it stays mounted and reachable for `duration.switch` under a trigger that already says `aria-expanded="false"`. The maintainer may want the contract line to read "hidden or inert", as the brief suggested.
- **P3-2.** `packages/perron/src/components/Accordion/Accordion.tsx:105` Two sibling components in the Layout category reach the heading-level override through different slot names: Accordion uses `slotProps.header.render` and Section uses `slotProps.title.render`. Each follows its own part's name, as the contract's Customising rule says. The api-consistency rule "one idea, one name" leaves this as a Direction: should the contract say which part a heading-level override targets?
- **P3-3.** `packages/perron/src/components/Accordion/Accordion.css:37` Each item's heading renders smaller than its own content. The trigger is `typography.label` (13 px, 600) and the panel is `typography.body` (14 px, 400), seen in `components-accordion--default` in light and dark. The contract's family text rule gives `typography.label` to rows and buttons, so this is right by the rules, but the maintainer may want a heading role for a disclosure heading.
- **P3-4.** `packages/perron/src/components/Accordion/Accordion.stories.tsx:33` The help answer "A pale dot hasn't reported for over a minute." describes a data state without the fixed data-state word content design gives it ("Ageing"). The content-design rule is that data states always use the table's words.
- **P3-5.** `packages/perron/src/components/Accordion/Accordion.tsx:17` The local type `Inherited<P, own>` is now copied into a fifth file, after Tabs, Toolbar, SegmentedControl and Menu. The shared-parts gate does not cover a type alias, but the Shared helpers rule's intent is one declaration. The maintainer may want it moved to `packages/perron/src/internal/`.
