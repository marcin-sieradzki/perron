## P2

- **P2-1.** `docs/components/slider.md:235` Several behaviours the spec promises have no `play` step that proves them, which breaks the contract's Stories rule ("a `play` test for every behaviour the component promises") and leaves these lines of the test-coverage checklist unticked: (1) the Keyboard row `Home`, `End` (`slider.md:169`): no story presses Home or End in a range, so nothing shows the upper thumb's Home and the lower thumb's End stopping at the other thumb; (2) the press-or-drag row (`slider.md:170`) and CR-9 (`slider.md:220`): `Pressed` never checks that a press focuses the thumb's input without `:focus-visible`, or that a key pressed afterwards brings the ring back; (3) the label row (`slider.md:171`): no story presses the label, so nothing shows that it focuses a single thumb's input and focuses nothing in a range; (4) CR-6 and the "Base UI already does" line (`slider.md:56`): `call()` drops the first argument, so nothing shows that `event` is `details.event` with `target.value` and `target.name`; (5) the reasons `onChangeCommitted` gives, which its Props row (`slider.md:80`) lists: only the value is checked for `'keyboard'` (`Default`) and `'drag'` (`Pressed`), a press and release on the track with no drag never checks its `'track-press'` commit, and neither handler is ever driven with `'input-change'`; (6) `slider.md:56`: nothing shows that `onChangeCommitted` stays uncalled when the value doesn't change (ArrowRight at `max`) or when `onChange` cancels (the cancel step in `Controlled` checks only the value); (7) CR-7 and checklist line `slider.md:187`: no story shows that a caller's `slotProps.thumb.getAriaLabel` or `getAriaValueText` replaces "Minimum" and "Maximum" or the formatted value; (8) CR-10: no story presses the control's padding to show that the thumb moves to the nearer end; (9) CR-12: `Disabled` never checks that a disabled thumb gets no hover or pressed layer; (10) CR-15: nothing checks that the thumb and fill have no transition or animation; (11) `slider.md:56`: nothing shows `thumbCollisionBehavior`'s default push between a range's thumbs, `data-touched` after a change, or a focused thumb losing focus when it becomes disabled. (The right-to-left arrows are left out of this list because of P3-1.)

## P3

- **P3-1.** `docs/components/slider.md:165` The Keyboard rows at 165 and 166 and the "Base UI already does" line at `slider.md:56` still say Base UI swaps the left and right arrows in right-to-left text. Base UI's `SliderThumb` reads direction only from its own `DirectionProvider`, and PerronProvider renders none, so on a page with `dir="rtl"` ArrowRight still raises the value (measured: 5 to 6). No Mylder locale is right-to-left, so only the spec's claim is wrong. The maintainer may want these lines to say a `DirectionProvider` is needed, or may want PerronProvider to carry one.
- **P3-2.** `packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.tsx:198` In a range, `getAriaLabel` names each thumb "Minimum" or "Maximum" alone. Once `aria-label` is set, Base UI drops the thumb's `aria-labelledby`, so the field's label is no longer part of the name, and two range sliders on one screen show up in a screen reader's list of controls as two "Minimum" sliders. The answer to question 9 binds this, but the maintainer may want each thumb named by the field's label together with the owned word.
- **P3-3.** `packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.css:78` The thumb's hover and pressed layers (`color.surface.hover`, then `color.surface.selected`) render at 5% and 9% alpha in light and 6% and 9% in dark, over a disc about 14 px across inside its edge (`components-slider--default`, both themes). This meets Pointer feedback as written, but the design is deferred, and the maintainer may want a stronger step on a part this small.
- **P3-4.** `docs/components/slider.md:53` The `Slider.Thumb` row says the thumb gets `data-index` "in a range", but Base UI also sets `data-index="0"` on a single thumb (measured in `components-slider--default`). It harms nothing, since no CSS reads it, but the line could drop "in a range".
- **P3-5.** `docs/components/sheet.md:214` This diff rewrote Sheet's Do and don't row, and it still asks callers to mark a horizontal carousel with a bare `data-base-ui-swipe-ignore`. Slider's CR-11 (`slider.md:222`) and its Don't row (`slider.md:262`) explain that a bare value drops the sheet's swipe for every touch that starts on the element. The maintainer may want the carousel advice to give `="x"`, as Slider's control does.
