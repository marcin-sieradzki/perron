# Review: Slider, round 4

## P1

None.

## P2

- **P2-1.** `docs/components/slider.md:246` Two behaviours the spec promises still have no `play` step, which breaks the contract's Stories rule ("a `play` test for every behaviour the component promises") and the spec's own claim at `slider.md:235` that every promised behaviour has a step in a `play` cell. These two lines of the test-coverage checklist are unticked: (1) the `locale` Props row (`slider.md:81`) and CR-13 (`slider.md:224`) promise that a caller's `locale` wins over the PerronProvider's, but no story gives `Slider` a `locale` prop, so only the provider's locale is ever exercised (rendering `components-slider--units` with `args=locale:sv` does give "1,5 km" in both sliders); (2) CR-8 (`slider.md:219`) and CR-13 say the sizers are formatted in `locale` and `format`, so the track keeps its width while the value moves, but the sizers are only read without a `format` and in English (`Default`, `Range`). `Units` never reads its sizers (measured "0.5 km" and "3 km" in English, "0,5 km" and "3 km" in Danish), and never checks that the track keeps its width as the figure goes from "1.5 km" to "2 km". The sizers come from Slider's own `useMemo` (`packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.tsx:178`), not from Base UI, so a break in that code would not be caught. Add these steps to the `Units` row and its `play`, or to another story.

## P3

- **P3-1.** `packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.tsx:198` In a range, `getAriaLabel` names each thumb "Minimum" or "Maximum" alone. Once `aria-label` is set, Base UI drops the thumb's `aria-labelledby`, so the field's label is no longer part of the thumb's name. In `components-slider--in-form`, the tree lists the range's thumbs as `slider "Minimum"` and `slider "Maximum"`, so two range sliders on one screen would show up in a screen reader's list of controls as two "Minimum" sliders. The answer to question 9 binds this, but the maintainer may want each thumb named by the field's label together with the owned word.
- **P3-2.** `packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.css:78` The thumb's hover and pressed layers (`color.surface.hover`, then `color.surface.selected`) render at 5% and 9% alpha in light and at 6% and 9% in dark, over a disc about 14 px across inside its edge (`components-slider--default`, both themes). This meets Pointer feedback as written, but the design is deferred, and the maintainer may want a stronger step on a part this small.
- **P3-3.** `packages/perron/src/components/Slider/Slider.css:47` The empty part of the track, `color.surface.inset` (rgb(235, 238, 235) in light), sits at about 1.2:1 against the white page, so where the range ends, and so how far the thumb is from `max`, is barely visible (`components-slider--default` light). The spec argues this from Elevation and Meter's track (`slider.md:115`), and the thumb and the fill pass 3:1. Since the design is deferred, the maintainer may want an edge or a stronger track so the range's end meets the 3:1 that Accessibility asks of a control's parts.
- **P3-4.** `docs/components/sheet.md:214` This diff rewrote Sheet's Do and don't row, and it still tells callers to mark a horizontal carousel with a bare `data-base-ui-swipe-ignore`. Slider's CR-11 (`slider.md:222`) and its Don't row (`slider.md:264`) explain, as Base UI's Drawer reference does, that a bare value drops the sheet's swipe for every touch that starts on the element. The maintainer may want the carousel advice to give `="x"`, as Slider's control does.
