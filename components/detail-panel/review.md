## P2

- **P2-1.** `packages/perron/src/patterns/DetailPanel/DetailPanel.tsx:154` The `ClassName` type and the `withClass` helper (lines 154-164) are a third copy of the helper already declared in `packages/perron/src/components/Sheet/Sheet.tsx:141-149` and `packages/perron/src/components/Dialog/Dialog.tsx:106-114`. This breaks the contract's Shared helpers rule ("A helper a second component needs joins this table first"). Because `withClass` reads no Base UI, it belongs in `packages/perron/src/internal/nodes.ts` beside `classes`, with a row in the Shared helpers table, and DetailPanel, Sheet and Dialog should all import it from there.
- **P2-2.** `docs/patterns/detail-panel.md:180` The spec says the header row "has room for two controls at most beside the title". It does not: with the spec's own recommended action, the title column is too narrow to keep words whole. In `patterns-detailpanel--with-action` (light and dark, 800 px) the title column is 108 px wide and "Next stop: Nørreport St." wraps. In `patterns-detailpanel--long-title` (light and dark) "Hovedbanegård" breaks as "Hovedbanegå / rd" and the close button floats to the middle of a 200 px header. At 320 px, the width a phone's bottom panel has, WithAction breaks "Husum" into "Hus / um" and "Nørreport" into "Nørre / port". This is a design-qa Polish finding on the Text line: rough although no rule is broken. Options for the spec and code: give `action` a row of its own under the title and subheader so the title takes the row beside the close button, or keep it in the row only above a width the spec names.
- **P2-3.** `docs/patterns/detail-panel.md:23` The spec changes DetailHeader's assumption and asks the build to rewrite only Sheet's `title` description. As a result, DetailHeader's spec (`docs/patterns/detail-header.md:19`, `:50`, `:63`, `:123`) and its shipped JSDoc (`packages/perron/src/patterns/DetailHeader/DetailHeader.tsx:41`, "such as its close button") still say DetailPanel puts the Sheet's close button in `action`. DetailPanel's CR-2 and its Do and don't (`docs/patterns/detail-panel.md:180`) now forbid exactly that. CR-2 holds, so the rewrite list on line 23 should include those DetailHeader lines and the JSDoc, with a changeset line for the JSDoc change.

## P3

- **P3-1.** `packages/perron/src/patterns/DetailPanel/DetailPanel.tsx:9` The `perron-allow(patterns)` comment gives as its reason that "the contract does not yet say whether a pattern may compose another". But the contract's Patterns section (`docs/components/README.md:269`) does list everything a pattern may import, and another pattern is not on that list. Meanwhile the registry's `dependsOn` (`packages/perron/components.json:363`) and CR-1 (`docs/patterns/detail-panel.md:148`) name DetailHeader. No rule settles which holds, so this needs the maintainer: either the Patterns line grows to allow the patterns an entry's `dependsOn` names, and the allowance goes, or the allowance's reason is corrected to say it departs from that line.
- **P3-2.** `packages/perron/src/patterns/DetailPanel/DetailPanel.tsx:167` The Sheet's title part is rendered as a `div`, so `.pn-Sheet-title`'s `typography.heading-md` font, colour and `overflow-wrap` are inherited by DetailHeader's `action` cell, which sets no font of its own. A plain-string `action` therefore renders as a 20 px semibold heading. A Button in `action` sets its own font and is unaffected. The maintainer may want the panel, or DetailHeader, to give `action` a typography role.
- **P3-3.** `packages/perron/src/patterns/DetailPanel/DetailPanel.tsx:56` The `children` JSDoc ends with design reasoning ("The body comes as free `children`, not as data … and the panel is their frame, as Sheet is."), and so does `anchor`'s (line 65, on why `left` is excluded). Both come from the spec's Props rows (`docs/patterns/detail-panel.md:56`, `:57`) and land in the docs page's props table. The maintainer may prefer that reasoning in the spec's prose, leaving the JSDoc to say what the prop does.
